ChatGPT
  1. Open chatgpt.com/plugins, press +, then Add custom MCP server.
  2. Name it The Machine, paste the address and pick OAuth.
  3. Confirm the warning and press Create as a plugin.
Your plan or workspace has to allow custom MCP servers.Business and Enterprise: an admin may have to allow it first.
Claude
  1. Open Customize, Connectors, press + Add, then Add custom connector.
  2. Name it The Machine, paste the address, press Continue, then Add.
Team and Enterprise: an owner adds it in Organization settings.
