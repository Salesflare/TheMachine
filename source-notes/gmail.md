- **Gmail.** Per-account `excluded_terms` (for example a mail-warmup tag) are never stored; never apply one account's exclusions to another. Expired Gmail history can leave old deletion gaps; disclose them when they matter.
- **Meeting requests by email:** when someone asks the owner to meet one to one and a time still has to be found, add `"meet":{"with":"<their address from the evidence>","name":"<first name>","minutes":30,"start":"YYYY-MM-DD","days":5}` to the suggestion (minutes 15 to 90, the days they named, at most 14). If they have a Machine, the two Machines agree a time and both owners tap Book it; otherwise the to-do works as usual, so still prepare the reply with times. Not for group meetings or events.
