- **Salesflare CRM.** Only colleagues' emails that don't involve the owner (their own come from Gmail) and meetings with notes are stored, each with its `crm_account` and `contacts`. A colleague's customer email shows how an account is going; it isn't a task for the owner by default. With `join_pipelines` configured, the connector adds the owner's own user to accounts linked to opportunities in those pipelines (a POST only, never another user, never PUT); joins are in `joined_accounts`, other inaccessible accounts in `inaccessible_accounts`.
- **After a restart.** Each round starts with the account lists ordered by last email and meeting date, which run close to Salesflare's 2-minute gateway limit on a large team. A release restarts a team's Machines together, so a Salesflare source with a finished round waits out its poll gap plus a random spread of up to 3 minutes (`SALESFLARE_RESTART_SPREAD`) instead of reading at once; one mid-round goes on at once. Teammates' rounds take about as long, so two Machines whose starts landed close stay in step for hours (both answered 504 in a regular round); each finished round adds a fresh spread of up to a minute (`SALESFLARE_ROUND_SPREAD`) to its poll gap.
