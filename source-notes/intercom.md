- **Intercom** (the `intercom` connector). Up to 500 parts per conversation, cuts flagged, no attachments. The first message is the customer's, unless it is written as a part: `opening` (a teammate or the bot wrote it) or `automated` (a campaign or another automatic message, nobody's promise); the customer is then who answers it. The collector only reads; that is no ban on Intercom work the owner accepts (`PREPARATION.md`; replies go out as an `intercom` Send button). A conversation assigned to a teammate (`assigned` in the review) is theirs: context only, no to-do; the owner's, nobody's or a team inbox's with no one assigned can be one. An open to-do made only from Intercom closes by itself once a teammate takes the conversation.
