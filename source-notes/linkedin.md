- **LinkedIn** (the `linkedin` connector). Messages from recently active LinkedIn conversations, read hourly in waking hours without marking them read. The title is the conversation; outgoing messages have **You** as sender. Sales pitches and connection spam are usually noise; a personal check-in from someone the owner knows is not (see Personal check-ins). A reply goes out as a `linkedin` Send button, or right away with `send_now:true` when the owner approved sending a draft they already saw.
