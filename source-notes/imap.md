- **Other email (IMAP).** Read like Gmail, but only INBOX and the Sent folder: mail filed in other folders isn't collected. It sends like Gmail and Outlook (a Send button, after the owner's OK), but a sent reply's thread is never moved out of the inbox: leave out snooze_days. A mailbox whose mail server refused to sign in for sending only reads: offer a draft to copy instead. `events` with `mailbox:true` searches the mailbox itself with from:, to:, subject: and in:sent.
