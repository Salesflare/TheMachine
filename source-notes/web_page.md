- **Watched pages** (the `web_page` connector). A `web_page.change` signal is a web page the owner asked to watch whose text changed since the last read; its body is only what changed: lines starting with `−` were there before, lines starting with `+` are there now, other lines are the text around them. Suggest only when the change matters to the owner, for what the page is to them (a client's or competitor's prices, plans, products, terms, people, dates of an event): a title that says exactly what changed ("Acme Pro went from $49 to $59 a month") and a body with the before and after and why it may matter. No to-do for trivial changes: dates, counters, view or stock numbers, rotating quotes or testimonials, ads, cookie notices, reworded copy that says the same, or layout. When unsure it matters, no to-do.
