## Open it

On a phone, tap the to-do's title. On a computer, use the to-do's chat link or **See in chat ›**. On a computer, clicking the title renames the to-do.

## Emails in the chat

Each email shows only what is new in it. The earlier messages it quotes are folded under **Earlier messages**. **Read the thread** opens the emails in full.

## What to ask

- About the thread, the people or the background: "What did they ask last time?"
- To change the draft: "Make it shorter" or "Mention the new date".

A question gets an answer and leaves the draft as it is. Approvals stay on the to-do's buttons: chat never sends a draft in the same reply that wrote it.

On a computer, Enter or Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac) sends and Shift+Enter starts a new line. On a phone, use the send button.

## When something is wrong

Say so in the chat, like "She's not the CEO" or "He left the company". The Machine fixes it where it came from: a role, a company, a title, a project's stage, the to-do's project, or a rule it learned. The chat then shows what changed, like **Fixed: Ana's role is Head of Sales, from her email signature**, with **Undo**. Undo puts back what was there, unless it changed since.

On a GBrain page, only The Machine's own section changes; what you wrote there stays yours. When it can't find where something came from, it learns from your note as before.

## Model and thinking

Under the message box, the chat shows its model and thinking level, like "GPT-6.1 Sol" and "Low thinking". Tap either to pick another for this chat only. Models are listed under their provider. New chats use what you set in **Settings**, **Model** (see [Model](model.md)).

**Fast**, at the end of the model menu, makes your to-do chats answer quicker, at a higher cost (about twice the price, or more of your plan). It applies to all your to-do chats, only to the messages you send and to Do it, never to work done in the background. Off by default. A model without a fast option shows it grayed out.

## Stop a reply

While a reply is being written, the send button turns into **Stop**. Stop ends it right away and keeps what it wrote so far, marked **Stopped**. Write a new message to carry on, or tap **Try again**.

## Add files

Tap the paperclip, paste a screenshot, or drop files on the chat. Each file shows above the message box, and × takes it back. A message can be only files.

- Images (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP) up to 5 MB go to the model as images.
- PDFs, text, CSV, JSON and Office files up to 10 MB are files The Machine reads, and can attach to a draft.
- Up to 5 files per message.

Files stay on your Machine, in its data folder, encrypted like your messages. Files you add but never send are removed after a day. A new to-do can carry files too, from **New to-do** (see [Make one yourself](cards.md#make-one-yourself)).
