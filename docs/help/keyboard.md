## The palette

Press `⌘K` on a Mac or `Ctrl K` on Windows and Linux, or `/`, to open **Search and commands**. Type part of a name: an action, a page, a to-do, a person, an organization, a project or a message. Under **Messages** it shows the best matches, and **See all results** opens the full list. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or Esc to close. Recent commands come first.

On a phone, tap the search icon in the top bar.

## Shortcuts

Press `?` for **Keyboard shortcuts**, the full list for your computer.

- Go to a page: `G` then `T` To-dos, `C` Calendar, `P` People, `O` Organizations, `J` Projects, `W` Who's who (once it shows), `F` Fleet, `L` Learning, `N` Connections, `,` Settings. `G` then `E` opens the message search results. The old `G` then `S` and `G` then `B` still work.
- `N` adds a new to-do. `T` starts Talk, or ends it while it's live. `J` and `K` (or `↓` and `↑`) move through a list, Enter opens.
- On a to-do: `E` renames it, `S` snoozes it, `D` marks it done, `R` writes in its chat.
- On the Calendar: `T` goes to today, `J` and `K` (or `→` and `←`) to the next or previous week or month. `A`, `W` and `M` switch to Agenda, Week and Month.
- On a computer, `⌘B` on a Mac or `Ctrl B` on Windows and Linux folds the sidebar to icons and back.

Keys don't work while typing in a field.

## Moving around

- Each page puts focus on its first item when you get there with the keyboard. On To-dos that's the first to-do, or the first list when every list is folded.
- `J` or `↓` goes to the next to-do, `K` or `↑` to the previous one. The head of a folded list is a stop too: Enter or `→` opens it, `←` folds it again.
- Enter on a to-do opens its chat, ready to type in. Esc leaves the box, and the next Esc closes the chat and goes back to the to-do. With an empty box, one Esc closes it.
- Esc also closes a dialog, the palette and the shortcut list, and puts focus back where it was.
- Tab moves through the buttons. Whatever has focus shows a ring.

## Nothing is sent from a key

Sending, approving or posting never happens from a key or straight from the palette. Entries like Review the email only open the draft, where it can be read, changed and sent with its own button.
