## The palette

Press `⌘K` on a Mac, `Ctrl K` on Windows and Linux, or `/` to open **Search and commands**. Type part of a name: an action, a page, a to-do, a person, an organization, a project or a message. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or Esc to close. Recent commands come first, and **See all results** opens every matching message.

On a phone, tap the search icon in the top bar.

## Ask a question

Type a question, like “what did Kim say about the pilot?”, and a short answer from your own mail and messages shows above the results, with a link to each message it used. It answers in the language you ask in, only from the area you're viewing (All, Work or Personal). A name or a few keywords just search.

Ask about someone and a time, like “what did Kim say last week?”, and it reads only the messages from or with that person on those days, in your time zone. If they sent nothing then, it says so and gives their latest message with its date.

It also reads what the Machine already keeps on the question: promises (open, or closed in the last 30 days), what it learned, the summary of the person, project or organization you ask about, and meeting notes. Each one the answer used shows under it, named as what it is (Promise, Learned, Summary, Meeting notes), and opens where it lives. Only what matches is read, from the area you're viewing.

If several people have that name, it says which one it means. The other messages it read show under Messages. The question an empty search suggests is only one it has messages for. The answer never sends anything or adds a to-do, and it needs a model (Settings, Model).

## Shortcuts

Press `?` for **Keyboard shortcuts**, the full list for your computer.

- Go to a page: `G` then `T` To-dos, `C` Calendar, `P` People, `O` Organizations, `J` Projects, `F` Fleet, `L` Learning, `N` Connections, `,` Settings. `G` then `E` opens the message search results.
- `N` adds a new to-do. `T` starts Talk, or ends it while it's live. `J` and `K` (or `↓` and `↑`) move through a list, Enter opens.
- On a to-do: `E` renames it, `S` snoozes it, `D` marks it done, `R` writes in its chat.
- On the Calendar: `T` goes to today, `J` and `K` (or `→` and `←`) to the next or previous week or month. `A`, `W` and `M` switch to Agenda, Week and Month.
- On a computer, `⌘B` on a Mac or `Ctrl B` on Windows and Linux folds the sidebar to icons and back.

Keys don't work while typing in a field.

## Moving around

- Arriving on a page with the keyboard puts focus on its first item.
- `J` or `↓` goes to the next to-do, `K` or `↑` to the previous one. The head of a folded list is a stop too: Enter or `→` opens it, `←` folds it again.
- Enter on a to-do opens its chat, ready to type in. Esc leaves the box, and the next Esc closes the chat and goes back to the to-do. With an empty box, one Esc closes it.
- Esc also closes a dialog, the palette and the shortcut list, and puts focus back where it was.
- Tab moves through the buttons. Whatever has focus shows a ring.

## Nothing is sent from a key

No key or palette entry sends, approves or posts anything. Entries like "Review the email" only open the draft, so you can read it, change it and send it with its own button. The one exception is [Go through](cards.md): there Enter sends the draft it shows in full.
