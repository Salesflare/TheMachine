Most of it happens by itself. Your Machine learns from what you do, so there is little to set up and nothing to build.

## It learns by itself

- **How you write**: from the messages you send yourself, per channel, so an email reads like your emails and a chat like your chats. Drafts follow it.
- What you want: from the to-dos you finish, dismiss or snooze, and why.
- Facts and habits: from what you say in a to-do's chat, like "I never take calls on Fridays".

About twice a day it makes a few small changes from this. **Feedback**, **What I've learned** shows each one, with **Undo**. See [Writing](writing.md#it-keeps-learning).

## Teach it faster

- Edit a draft before you send it. It learns from what you changed.
- Tap **Do it differently**, or **Ask for changes** on a draft, and say what you'd like.
- Tap **Not needed** and pick why.
- Tell it in a to-do's chat: "shorter, and no exclamation marks". It shows **Saved to How you write** when it took it.

## What you can set

In **Settings**:

- **Writing**: your signatures, and the lines on how you write per channel. Change any line in your own words. See [Writing](writing.md).
- **Notifications**: what reaches you, and where. See [Notifications](notifications.md).
- **Work hours**: when it may message you. See [Work hours](work-hours.md).
- **Security**: Standard or Careful, for how much shows on your phone and how long things are kept. See [Security levels](security-levels.md).
- **Language and region**: the language it uses with you, and how dates read. See [Settings](settings.md#general).

What your team sees is in [Sharing rules](sharing-rules.md) and [Security and privacy](security-and-privacy.md).

## Skills, for your own steps

A skill is for a task with steps of your own, like "how we answer pricing questions". You don't need one for your writing style or preferences: those it learns by itself. See [Skills](skills.md).
