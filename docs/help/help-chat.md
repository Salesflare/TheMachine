Tap **Help** at the top, beside **Settings**, and ask in your own words, like "How do I snooze a to-do?". Press Enter to ask, or Shift+Enter for a new line. Help answers in the language you write in.

## Where answers come from

Help reads the help pages that match your question, and the app's own source code for what they leave open. Each answer links to the pages it used, so you can read more.

It runs on your Machine's own model as a short, separate chat. Only your question and the matching pages go in: none of your messages, people or settings.

## What it can't do

Help answers questions. It can't see your data, and it changes nothing: it tells you where to tap instead.

When something looks broken, open **Report a bug** in the same panel. See [Get support](get-support.md#report-a-bug).
