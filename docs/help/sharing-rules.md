## Set them

Decide what your Machine tells your Fleet. In the **Fleet** tab, open **Sharing**. Each kind of question has a rule:

- **Ask me first**: you see each question and decide.
- **Always**: your Machine answers by itself.
- **Never**: it says no.
- **Names**: whether the answer may say who, like a guest's name, or leaves names out.

It never shares email content or what your meetings are about. **Different for someone? Add an exception** sets a rule for one person.

Rules change only in the dashboard, never from a message.

## In a Work Fleet

In a Standard Work Fleet, who knows someone, meeting context and who handles shared emails have just **Always** and **Never**. **Always** answers with only what the [Team Graph](team-graph.md) already shares with your team, and names only people it shares. A rule saved as **Ask me first** works as **Always**.

While the Team Graph isn't sharing, who knows someone and meeting context keep **Ask me first** too, and so does a Careful Fleet. See [Fleets](fleet.md#answered-by-itself-in-a-work-fleet).

## The first time someone asks

A question that needs you comes as a to-do, with **Share once**, **Always share**, **Decline** or **Never share**.
