## Set them

In the **Fleet** tab, open Sharing. Each kind of question has a rule:

- Ask me first: you see each question and decide.
- Always: your Machine answers by itself.
- Never: it declines.
- Names: only names, no details.

It never shares email content, or what your meetings are about. "Different for someone? Add an exception" sets a rule for one person.

Rules change only on the dashboard, never from a message.

## In a Work Fleet

In a Standard Work Fleet, who knows someone, meeting context and who handles shared emails have two rules: **Always** and **Never**. Always answers with only what the [Team Graph](team-graph.md) shares with your team, names only of people it already shares. A rule saved as Ask me first works as Always.

While the Team Graph isn't sharing, who knows someone and meeting context keep all three rules. A Careful Fleet keeps Ask me first. See [Fleets](fleet.md#answered-by-itself-in-a-work-fleet).

## The first time someone asks

A question that needs you comes as a to-do, with **Share once**, **Always share**, **Decline** or **Never share**.
