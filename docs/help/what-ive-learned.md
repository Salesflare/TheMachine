When the first look ends, **Here’s what I’ve learned** shows under your to-dos. It fills in while the rest of setup finishes, and stays for a day or until you close it.

## What it shows

- **How you write**: the first lines of how you write email.
- **People you work with most**: up to 6 people you write with on your work accounts, with what you work on together, from a project or a recent thread.
- **Your usual hours**: when you send work mail.

Only work, never anything personal. A part with too little to go on doesn't show.

## Fix what's off

Tap **Not right** next to anything, type how it should read and tap **Save**. **Remove** takes it off. Each fix shows under **Learning**, **Changes**, with **Undo**.

Your usual hours don't change your [Work hours](work-hours.md). How you write is also in [Writing](writing.md).
