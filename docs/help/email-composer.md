## Open it

Click an email address, on a person's page in Graph or as a link in a to-do or a chat. **New email** opens, to that address, with an empty subject and message. On a phone it fills the screen.

**From** starts on the mailbox for that person: your Work mailbox for a work contact, your Personal one for family and friends. Click it to pick another of your mailboxes, or a confirmed alias.

## Send it

Write the subject and the message, with bold, lists and links if you like. Nothing goes out until you press **Send now**.

The email then goes out like any draft you approve: it shows as sent on its own to-do, on the person's page and in your mailbox. If it can't go out, the to-do keeps it, ready to send from there.

Escape or **Cancel** closes it. What you typed is not kept.

## Your own mail app

**Open in email** opens your own mail app instead, with what you typed so far. A click on the address with Ctrl or Cmd held does that too.

Only mailboxes with **Can do things** on can send. Without one, a click on an address opens your mail app, as before.
