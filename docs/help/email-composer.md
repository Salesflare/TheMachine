## Open it

Click an email address, on a person's page in Graph or as a link in a to-do or a chat. **New email** opens, to that address, with an empty subject and message. On a phone it fills the screen.

**From** starts on the mailbox for that person: your Work mailbox for a work contact, your Personal one for family and friends. Click it to pick another of your mailboxes, or a confirmed alias.

## Send it

Write the subject and the message, with bold, lists and links if you like. Nothing goes out until you press **Send now**. Or press **Send later** and pick when it goes: see [Send later](send-later.md).

The email then goes out like any draft you approve: it shows as sent on its own to-do, on the person's page and in your mailbox. If it can't go out, the to-do keeps it, ready to send from there.

## If you don't send it

Close it with Escape, × or Back and what you typed is kept, with the subject, From and who gets it. It never sends by itself.

- A new email waits above your to-dos as **Unsent email to**, with **Continue** and **Discard**.
- An edited draft says **Edited, not sent** on its to-do, on every channel. Open it again and your text is there. A follow-up you edit in **Review** works the same.
- After a reload, or when your phone closed the app, the window you were typing in opens again.

**Discard** in the window throws away what you typed, and **Undo** brings it back. A message you sent, scheduled or discarded doesn't come back.

If the draft changed meanwhile, you see the new draft and **Use it** shows what you typed. Neither replaces the other until you send. Two tabs with different text work the same way.

**New to-do**, **Ask for changes**, an answer to your Machine and a review for someone else keep what you typed too. **Cancel** throws it away.

What you typed stays in this browser only, encrypted, for 30 days. It goes when you sign out or someone else signs in. Other devices don't see it. Without https it lasts while the page is open.

## Your own mail app

**Open in email** opens your own mail app instead, with what you typed so far. A click on the address with Ctrl or Cmd held does that too.

Only mailboxes with **Can do things** on can send. Without one, a click on an address opens your mail app, as before.
