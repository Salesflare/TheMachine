A skill teaches your Machine how to do one kind of task, like "answer pricing questions". It's instructions only: it never changes what your Machine is allowed to do.

## Write one

In **Settings**, **Skills**, press **New skill** or **Start from an example**. Give it a **Name**, **When to use it** and the **Instructions**, then **Save**.

## Import

Press **Import**:

- **From Claude** or **From ChatGPT**, then **Find my skills**. It looks in that account, in your Machine's browser.
- Or bring one yourself: **Upload .skill or .zip**, **Upload a folder** or **Paste a SKILL.md**.

Pick the ones you want and press **Import selected**. Only instructions come in; scripts never do.

## Search ClawHub

Find skills other people publish. Read one before you install it: they're made by others, not The Machine.

## From your notes

When many of your notes are about one kind of task, a to-do suggests a skill. It checks your skills first:

- One already does that job: **Update <skill>** shows that skill with what the notes teach, changes highlighted. Only **Update skill** changes it.
- One is related but separate: the to-do says how in one line and still offers **Create skill**.

**Not now** keeps the notes as they are. Skills your Machine proposes in Settings > Skills get the same check.

## From a to-do

Ask for a skill in a to-do, and your Machine writes it there. Open **Read the skill**, then press **Create skill** (or **Update skill** for one you have). Nothing is installed before you do. Dismissed it in Settings > Skills instead? The to-do says **Skill dismissed** and stays open for you to close.

## Share one

Share a skill with a friend or teammate. They read it and choose whether to install it. Later edits reach them only if you share again.
