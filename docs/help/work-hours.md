In **Settings**, **Notifications**, under **Work hours**:

- **Work days**: work to-dos and snoozed work wait for a work day.
- **Messages from** and **Until**: nothing is sent to you outside these hours, not push, Telegram or email.
- **Snoozes land at**: when a snooze of a day or more comes back.
- **Morning digest**: when the morning summary comes.
- **Time zone**: yours, for all of the above.

Press **Save**. By default it's Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 23:00, snoozes at 09:00 and the digest at 08:30.

What goes out within these hours, and how, is in [Notifications](notifications.md).

On your own computer, `assistant hours` shows and changes them too.
