## Make one

In **New to-do**, pick **Repeats** and a rhythm: **Daily**, **Weekdays**, weekly, monthly on a date or on a weekday like the first Monday, or yearly. **Custom…** takes your own words, like every 2 weeks or every last Friday.

Or just say it: "every first Monday of the month do payroll" in New to-do or in Talk makes a to-do that repeats.

Workdays count too: "send invoices every second workday of the month" or "close the books on the last workday of the month". A workday is Monday to Friday, without your country's public holidays.

When the words aren't clear, like "every 2nd business day" (the 2nd of the month, or every other day?), the to-do keeps all your words and doesn't repeat. Pick **Repeats** to set it.

For a to-do you already have, **Repeat…** under **…**, or say "repeat this every week" in its chat.

## How it repeats

The to-do shows a short line, like "Repeats weekly on Monday". Only one is open at a time: the next comes on its date once this one is done or not needed.

A missed one isn't stacked. It stays open and shows how many dates it missed, and when you finish it the next one comes on the next date from today.

Snoozing moves only this one. The rhythm stays the same.

Done before its date? It counts for what's due now, and that date still comes for the period after.

Each one is prepared like any to-do, knowing how the last one went.

It also keeps how it's done: the steps it worked out and what you corrected, so the next one doesn't need them again. Its chat says **Saved how to do this next time**, with **Edit** and **Undo**. It never sends anything by itself.

## Change or stop it

Tap the "Repeats" line on the to-do: pick another rhythm, **Skip this one** (it closes this one and brings the next on its date) or **Stop repeating**. "Stop repeating" in its chat works too. Stopping keeps the open to-do; only the next ones don't come.

## Suggestions

When your Machine learns about something you do on a rhythm, or sees the same to-do done on a regular schedule, it asks once: **Make this a recurring to-do?** **Make it recurring** or **No thanks**. It never makes one by itself, and nothing is sent by itself.
