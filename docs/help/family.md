## What it does

- Find a time: "When are we all free on Saturday?" Only busy times are shared.
- To-dos: ask someone in your family to do something. They accept or decline.
- Plans: propose one. Everyone answers I'm in or Can't make it, and gets a link to add it to their calendar. Nothing is written to anyone's calendar for them.

No work questions go through a Family Fleet.

## Invite suggestions

When a to-do is about family plans with someone not in your Family yet, like "Who picks up the kids on Wednesday?", it can suggest inviting them.

- **Invite** opens the invite form with their address filled in, and shows the email they'd get. Nothing is sent without you.
- **Not now** hides it for 90 days.
- Only for people who are family, by your Graph or your own words. Never for Work contacts, people already in your Family, or someone with an open invite. At most once per person.
- Turn it off in Settings, General, under Fleet: **Suggest inviting family**.

## Rules

By default, free and busy times are shared, and to-dos and plans ask you first. Change it on the Sharing pane: see [Sharing rules](sharing-rules.md).

A Family Fleet is free. A hosted Machine in it costs the same as any hosted Machine: see [Plans and billing](plans-and-billing.md).
