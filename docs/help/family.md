## What it does

- When everyone's free: "When are we all free on Saturday?" Busy times only.
- To-dos: ask someone in your family to do something. They accept or decline.
- Plans: propose a plan. Each one answers I'm in or Can't make it, with a link to add it to their own calendar. Nothing is written to anyone's calendar.

No work questions go through a Family Fleet.

## Invite suggestions

When a to-do is about family plans with someone who isn't in your Family yet, like "Who picks up the kids on Wednesday?", it can ask: "Invite Sasha? Your Machines can sort this out without you asking each other."

- **Invite** opens the invite form on your account page with their address filled in. The email they'd get is shown before anything goes. Nothing is sent without you.
- **Not now** hides it for 90 days.
- Only for people who are family as your Graph or your own words say. Never for Work contacts, people already in your Family, or someone with an open invite. At most once per person.
- Turn it off in Settings, Fleet: **Suggest inviting family**.

## Rules

By default, free and busy times are shared; to-dos and plans ask first. Change it on the Sharing pane: see [Sharing rules](sharing-rules.md).

A Family Fleet is free. Hosted Machines in it are paid like any hosted Machine: see [Plans and billing](plans-and-billing.md).
