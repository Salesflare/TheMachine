## Send an email later, at a set time

Press **Send later** next to **Send now** and pick a time. It goes out exactly as you approved it, and if they write first it waits for you again. See [Send later](send-later.md). LinkedIn and X messages can't wait: **Snooze** the to-do and press **Send now** when it comes back.

## Take back a sent email or message

Not possible once it went out. Nothing goes before your yes, so read it in **Review and send** first. Some can still be stopped: **Cancel** on one that [goes later](send-later.md), in [Talk live](talk-live.md), **Undo** shows for a few seconds after you said yes, and **Stop sending** keeps back the rest of a row of [follow-ups](follow-ups.md).

## Where a draft is

A draft stays on its to-do. If it's no longer under **For you**, look under **Waiting on others**, **Coming back later**, **Older** or **Done / dismissed**, or find the to-do with **Search**. Older versions are in the to-do's chat, marked **Earlier draft**. A new email you didn't send waits above your to-dos (see [Write a new email](email-composer.md)).

## Light or dark

The dashboard is dark, unless **Light mode** is on. Turn it off for dark, or on for light, in **Settings** › **General**, under **This device**. It counts for that browser or phone only. With a keyboard, type "dark" or "light" in **Search and commands**.

## Fewer to-dos

Press **Not needed** on what you don't want to see, and pick why: your Machine learns from it. Or tell it in a to-do's chat, like "I never need these". To stop a whole source, open **Connections**, its **Details**, and turn off **Brings in signals**. **Dismiss all** clears everything under **Older** at once.

## To-dos from one newsletter or sender

Say so in the chat of one of its to-dos, like "Nothing from this mailing list needs a to-do". It says **Saved to what I’ve learned**, and later checks go by it. **Learning** lists it, with **Edit** and **Remove**.

The mail still comes in. To stop that too, unsubscribe, yourself or with [Newsletter cleanup](routines.md#templates).

## Who can see your to-dos

Only you. Your Machine is yours alone, and our support tools can't open your messages. Someone else sees a to-do only when you share it with them, like with **Hand off…**. A Work Fleet sees the client work your [Team Graph](team-graph.md) shares, never what's personal. [Sharing rules](sharing-rules.md) set what your Machine may answer.

## Share a to-do

Share it with a colleague or teammate: on the to-do, open **…**, then **Hand off…**. It needs a [Fleet](fleet.md), and a Work to-do goes to your Work Fleet only.

- **A teammate**: you pass it on, and it's theirs once they accept. They get only that to-do. See [Handoffs](handoffs.md).
- **Ask to review**: they look at it and edit the draft, and it stays yours. Family can review Personal ones. See [Ask to review](ask-to-review.md).
- **A teammate**, then **By email**: for someone without The Machine, with a page to answer on if you like. See [Task pages](task-pages.md).

To ask Family to do something, use your Family's pane on the **Fleet** tab.

## Forward an email to it

There's no address to forward to, and no need for one: it reads the mailboxes you connect. For something elsewhere, press **New to-do** and attach the message or a file, or share it to The Machine from your phone. See [Mobile app](mobile-app.md#share-to-the-machine).

## Out of office and automatic replies

Not possible: The Machine doesn't set an automatic reply. Set it in Gmail or Outlook itself. The Machine keeps drafting while you're away, and the drafts wait for you.

## Book, order or pay for something

[Do it](do-it.md) can look things up, pick and fill in the form in your Machine's browser, like a table or a ticket. The step that books or pays waits for you. A bill to pay from your bank comes as a to-do with the payment details, and you pay it yourself.

## Join a call or a meeting

Not possible: The Machine doesn't sit in on calls. It prepares you before a meeting and reads the summary or transcript after, from [Zoom](zoom.md), [Google Meet](google-meet.md), [Teams](teams.md) or [Granola](granola.md).

## Print

The Machine has no print button. Use your browser's own print for the page you see.
