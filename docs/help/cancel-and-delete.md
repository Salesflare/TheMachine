## Cancel

1. In **Settings** › **Account**, press **Cancel plan**, then **Cancel subscription**.
2. Confirm. It ends with the month you paid for. Cancel in the free days and you pay nothing.

Changed your mind? **Keep my subscription** takes it back. In the EU you can also withdraw within 14 days with **Withdraw from contract here**.

## Your data after you cancel

It's kept for {grace_days} days so you can export it, then deleted, backups included. To keep using it on your own computer, see [Move to your own computer](moving.md).

## Delete everything now

In **Settings** › **Account**, press **Delete account**, type DELETE or your email and press **Wipe everything now**. It ends your subscription, makes your Machine forget its Google and Microsoft sign-ins, and deletes your Machine, its backups and all its data. This can't be undone.

Without a hosted Machine, the button is **Delete my account**. A Machine on your own computer stays as it is.
