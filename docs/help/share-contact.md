## Share someone

On a person's page, open **⋯** and pick **Share contact…**. Choose who it goes to: a teammate in your Work Fleet, a friend, or someone in your Family. It goes to one person at a time.

Tick what goes: the name always, then any of their email addresses and phone numbers, their organization and their role. Add a short note on **Why you two should talk** if you like.

## See it before it's sent

**Preview** shows exactly what the other person gets. Nothing goes until you press **Send**. Change anything and it asks you to look again.

Nothing is ever shared by itself. It's sealed end to end for the other person's Machine, the way everything on [The Machine Network](fleet.md) is.

## Personal contacts

A person marked Personal, or one you keep private from your team, needs one more tick before **Preview**: "Share it anyway." Leave it unticked and nothing goes.

## Share your own contact

On **About you**, pick **Share my contact**. Choose which of your addresses and numbers go, the same way.

## When someone shares with you

It waits under **Shared with you** at the top of People, with who sent it and their note. Pick **Add** to put it in your Graph, or **Dismiss**.

- If you already know that person (the same email or number), Add only adds what's new to them. Their name, role and side stay yours.
- From a teammate, the person lands as Work, marked "Shared by" them. From a friend or Family, it's a suggestion to add, and the side is decided as for anyone else.
- The sender sees whether you added it, but nothing else of your Graph.
