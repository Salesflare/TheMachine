## Snooze a to-do

Press **Snooze** on a to-do. The menu offers a few times, like in 2 hours, tomorrow or in a week. For another moment, pick **Choose date** and **Time**, then **Save time**. The menu names the to-do and when it's due. With a deadline a few days off, it also offers a moment before it, like "Before it's due · Wed 9:00". You can still pick any time, also past the deadline.

The message after it says until when, like "Snoozed until tomorrow 08:00". Snoozed by mistake? **Undo** in it brings the to-do right back.

Snoozes of a day or more land at your "Snoozes land at" time, and work to-dos wait for a work day. Set both in [Work hours](work-hours.md).

Or say it in **Ask for changes**, like "remind me about this next Tuesday".

## When it comes back

It comes back as a notification on each device where notifications are on, and on Telegram if you use it, as **Reminders** in [Notifications](notifications.md) allow. The message says where. With neither, it's back on the dashboard, with **Turn on notifications** right there. On iPhone and iPad, get the [phone app](mobile-app.md) for notifications.

A to-do The Machine already finished comes back as it was. It works on it again only when something new came in while it was snoozed.

To have the message itself go out at a time, use [Send later](send-later.md).

## While it's snoozed

The to-do sits under **Coming back later**, and on its day in the [Calendar](calendar.md). **Unsnooze** brings it back now, **Change time** picks another time.

On Outlook, the email thread moves to a Snoozed folder until the to-do comes back. See [Microsoft](microsoft.md).
