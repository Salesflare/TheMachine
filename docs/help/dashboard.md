## The tabs

- **Suggestions**: your to-dos. See [To-dos](cards.md).
- **Calendar**: the week ahead with your meetings, due to-dos and promises. See [Calendar](calendar.md).
- **Events**: everything your Machine has read, to search.
- **Graph**: the people, organizations and projects in your messages. See [Graph](graph.md).
- **Fleet**: your team, family and friends on The Machine Network, once you have one. See [Fleet](fleet.md).
- **Feedback**: how the to-dos are doing, and what it learned.
- **Connections**: your sources and how they're doing. See [Connections](connections.md).

**Settings** is the gear at the top. **Find a to-do** searches your to-dos by title.

**Browser**, at the bottom of the sidebar, shows your Machine's own browser. On a computer it opens beside the sidebar, and so do **View in browser** on a to-do and **Open the browser** when a site needs a sign-in. A narrow window, like the app on half the screen, shows it under the top bar, not in a second window. On a phone it opens in a new tab.

The browser's front window shows in the middle and the rest of the screen is dark; a button in the corner shows the whole screen. The view fits again when the window changes size. **Full size** gives it the whole window; press it again to go back. The sign-in from a to-do has **Full size** too.

When the to-dos don't fit, the whole sidebar scrolls as one column, to-dos included, like the menu on a phone. The installed app does the same.

## New versions

When a new version of the dashboard is ready, it never reloads while you're using it. Opening or reloading the app loads the newest version. An open window loads it once the window is in the background, or after a few minutes without input, and never while something you typed isn't saved or sent, or a dialog is open. **Update ready**, at the top, loads it right away. A restart of your Machine only reconnects.

## Events

Search everything that came in, by meaning or by words, across all accounts or one. Each result opens in a reader, with a link to open it where it came from.

## Feedback

- **How the to-dos are doing**: over the last 30 days, how many were **Worth it**, done on the **First try**, **Finished**, and **Sent as is**.
- **What I've learned**: how you write, facts and how you like things done, from your notes. **Edit** any of it in your own words, and **Remove** anything that's no longer true. When a note on a to-do teaches a fact or rule, the to-do's chat says so under the reply (**Saved to what I’ve learned**, with **Edit** and **Undo**); that line comes from what was stored, so a reply never just claims it.
- **What you decided**: a timeline of your choices on to-dos.
