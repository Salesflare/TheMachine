## The tabs

- **To-dos**: your to-dos and promises. See [To-dos](cards.md).
- **Calendar**: the week ahead with your meetings, due to-dos and promises. See [Calendar](calendar.md).
- **Graph**: the people, organizations and projects in your messages, and **Who's who** when roles wait for your word. See [Graph](graph.md).
- **Fleet**: your team, family and friends on The Machine Network, once you have one. See [Fleet](fleet.md).
- **Learning**: how the to-dos are doing, and what it learned.
- **Connections**: your sources and how they're doing. See [Connections](connections.md).

**Search**, at the top of the sidebar, finds to-dos first, then people, messages and more. **Browser**, **Help** and **Settings** are at the bottom of the sidebar. On a phone, the menu button in the top bar opens the sidebar.

**Browser** shows your Machine's own browser. On a computer it opens beside the sidebar, and so do **View in browser** on a to-do and **Open the browser** when a site needs a sign-in. A narrow window, like the app on half the screen, shows it under the top bar, not in a second window. On a phone it opens in a new tab.

The browser's front window shows in the middle and the rest of the screen is dark; a button in the corner shows the whole screen. The view fits again when the window changes size. **Full size** gives it the whole window; press it again to go back. The sign-in from a to-do has **Full size** too.

**Stop** shows at the top while a to-do's run is using the browser, with that to-do's name beside **Browser**. One press ends the run right away, without asking: nothing more is clicked, typed or sent, and the to-do's chat says "Stopped from the browser".

With more than one run, it stops the one on the tab in front. On a phone the browser's tab has the same **Stop** at the top. To carry on, write in the chat or press **Try again**.

When the to-dos don't fit, the whole sidebar scrolls as one column, to-dos included, like the menu on a phone. The installed app does the same.

## New versions

When a new version of the dashboard is ready, it never reloads while you're using it. Opening or reloading the app loads the newest version. An open window loads it once the window is in the background, or after a few minutes without input, and never while something you typed isn't saved or sent, or a dialog is open. **Update ready**, at the top, loads it right away. A restart of your Machine only reconnects.

## Messages

Open search with `⌘K` on a Mac or `Ctrl K` on Windows and Linux, or the search icon in the top bar on a phone. Type two letters or more: **Messages** shows the best matches from everything your Machine read, by meaning or by words.

**See all results** opens the full list, for all accounts or one. Each result opens in a reader, with a link to where it came from. An empty search shows the newest messages.

## Learning

Learning has a row of tabs. A tab with nothing in it yet is left out.

- **What I've learned**: how you write, facts and how you like things done, from your notes. **Edit** any of it in your own words, and **Remove** anything that's no longer true. When a note on a to-do teaches a fact or rule, the to-do's chat says so under the reply (**Saved to what I’ve learned**, with **Edit** and **Undo**); that line comes from what was stored, so a reply never just claims it.
- A fact can say what it is (**Temporary**, **Commitment**, **Preference**) and **Until** when it holds. A temporary one fades in weeks, a preference or commitment in months, other facts in a year. Facts about someone have no limit; other facts stop at 80. Past its date a fact moves to **Ended**, where **Bring back** restores it.
- **Changes**: what it picked up from your edits, dismissals and chats, each with **Undo**. See [Writing](writing.md#it-keeps-learning).
- **Decisions**: a timeline of your choices on to-dos.
- **Shared**: learnings to share with The Machine team, and votes waiting for you. Shows only when something waits. See [Share learnings](share-learnings.md).
- **How it's going**: the last 30 days, and higher is better.
  - **Worth it**: of the to-dos you decided, how many you acted on (**Do it**, sent or done) instead of dismissing.
  - **First try**: of the to-dos you started with **Do it**, how many needed no correction afterward.
  - **Finished**: of the to-dos you started with **Do it**, how many finished without you asking it to keep going or try again.
  - **Sent as is**: of the drafts you sent, how many went without an edit or **Change** first.
  - Each number says how it compares with the 30 days before. **Worth it, week by week** shows the last 8 weeks, one bar per week, once there are two weeks with decisions; a mark under a week means rules changed that week.
  - **Worth it per source**: where the to-dos came from, like **Email**, **Added by you** (made with **New to-do**), **Phone notifications** or **The Machine itself** (problems in The Machine, like an error), and how many you acted on.
