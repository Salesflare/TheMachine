## The tabs

- **To-dos**: your to-dos and promises. See [To-dos](cards.md).
- **Calendar**: the week ahead with your meetings, due to-dos and promises. See [Calendar](calendar.md).
- **Graph**: the people, organizations and projects in your messages, and **Who's who** when roles wait for your word. See [Graph](graph.md).
- **Fleet**: your team, family and friends on The Machine Network, once you have one. See [Fleet](fleet.md).
- **Learning**: how the to-dos are doing, and what it learned.
- **Connections**: your sources and how they're doing. See [Connections](connections.md).

**Search**, at the top of the sidebar, finds to-dos, people, messages and more. **Browser**, **Help** and **Settings** sit at the bottom. On a phone, the menu button in the top bar opens the sidebar.

**Browser** shows your Machine's own browser, the one it uses for sites like LinkedIn. On a computer it opens beside the sidebar, as do **View in browser** on a to-do and **Open the browser** when a site needs a sign-in. On a phone it opens in a new tab.

**Full size** gives the browser the whole window. Press it again to go back.

While a to-do is using the browser, **Stop** shows at the top. One press ends that run at once: nothing more is clicked, typed or sent, and the to-do's chat says "Stopped from the browser".

With more than one run, it stops the one in front. To carry on, write in the to-do's chat or press **Try again**.

When the to-dos don't fit, the whole sidebar scrolls as one column.

On a phone, a dialog like **Send this email?** fills the screen, with its buttons always in reach, also while you type.

## Get more out of it

A small to-do at the top of To-dos lists what makes The Machine more useful: the phone app, notifications, WhatsApp, the app on this computer, another account and more connections. Each row has one button for that step, and ticks itself off once done.

Rows show only where they make sense. The × on a row closes it on every device. The × at the top closes the whole to-do.

**Undo** or **Show 1 hidden** brings a row back. To bring back the whole to-do, use **Settings**, **General**, **Setup to-do**. **Settings**, **General**, **This device** has the same controls, including **Get the app**.

## New versions

A new version of the dashboard never reloads while you're using it. It loads when you open the app, or when the window has been in the background or idle for a few minutes. It never interrupts unsaved text or an open dialog. **Update ready**, at the top, loads it right away.

## Messages

Open search with `⌘K` on a Mac or `Ctrl K` on Windows and Linux, or the search icon on a phone. Type two letters or more: **Messages** shows the best matches from everything your Machine read, by meaning or by words. Older [history](history.md) matches by words.

**See all results** opens the full list, for all accounts or one. Each result opens in a reader, with a link to where it came from. An empty search shows the newest messages.

## Learning

Learning shows what The Machine learned from you and how well its to-dos work. Empty tabs are hidden.

- **What I've learned**: how you write, facts, and how you like things done, from your notes. **Edit** any of it in your own words, and **Remove** what's no longer true. When a note teaches a fact or rule, the to-do's chat says **Saved to what I’ve learned**, with **Edit** and **Undo**.
- A fact can be **Temporary**, a **Commitment** or a **Preference**, with **Until** for when it holds. Temporary facts fade in weeks, preferences and commitments in months, other facts in a year. Past its date a fact moves to **Ended**, where **Bring back** restores it.
- **Changes**: what it picked up from your edits, dismissals and chats, each with **Undo**. See [Writing](writing.md#it-keeps-learning).
- **Decisions**: a timeline of your choices on to-dos.
- **Shared**: learnings to share with The Machine team, and votes waiting for you, when there are any. See [Share learnings](share-learnings.md).
- **How it's going**: four numbers for the last 30 days. Higher is better.
  - **Worth it**: of the to-dos you decided, how many you acted on (**Do it**, sent or done) instead of dismissing.
  - **First try**: of the to-dos you started with **Do it**, how many needed no correction afterward.
  - **Finished**: of the to-dos you started with **Do it**, how many finished without you asking it to keep going or try again.
  - **Sent as is**: of the drafts you sent, how many went without an edit or **Change** first.
  - Each number compares with the 30 days before. The chart shows a bar per day for the first two weeks, then per week, up to 8 weeks.
  - **Worth it per source**: how many you acted on per source, like **Email**, **Added by you** (from **New to-do**), **Phone notifications** or **The Machine itself** (problems like an error).
  - **Missed to-dos**: once a week The Machine checks a sample of messages that got no to-do, and estimates how many likely needed one. It lists the ones it found: **Open** shows the message, and **Real miss** or **Not a miss** tells it whether it was right. Your answers make the number more accurate and stay on your Machine. Shows after the first weekly check.
