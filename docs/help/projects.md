Your Machine names projects from what your messages are about. Messages that look like they belong show as "probably part of it". Renaming a project to another project's name joins the two. Or run them by hand, like deals in a CRM.

## Add a project

**New project** at the top of a board, **+ Add** under a stage, **New project** at the top of the list, **+** in the top bar on a phone, or from a person's or organization's page.

Give it a name, a board and stage, an organization (search your Graph, or **Add** a new one), a value, an expected close date, people and notes. Projects already in your Graph with a name like it show first.

## Edit, win or lose a project

- **Edit** on a project's page, or **Edit** in its **⋯** on the board (or press E), changes all of the above.
- **Won** or **Lost**, with why. Won moves it to the board's last stage, Lost to Stalled or declined. **Reopen** undoes it.
- **Archive** takes it off the board and lists; a new message about it brings it back.
- **Delete** removes it for good; its messages and people stay.

## Description

One or two sentences under a project's name: what it is and where it stands. Your Machine writes it from the project's messages, to-dos, promises, facts and stage, and updates it when something changes, like a new message, a stage move, or a win or loss. With nothing specific to say yet, it shows none.

**Edit** under it, or **Edit description** in the **⋯** menu, makes it yours: "Edited by you", and your Machine leaves it alone. **Keep it updated** hands it back. Saving it empty shows none.

On a shared Work project your team sees the description only when everything it's based on went to them too. Your own edit stays with you.

## Move a project

Drag it to another stage, or on a phone use **⋯**, **Move to**. Your move stands until a newer message moves it.

**Stages**, at the top of a board, shows only the stages you pick, per board. **Clear** shows them all again.

"4 with no reply in 10+ days" under a stage: tap it to show only those, on the board and the list. Tap again to show all.

## A project's to-dos

A to-do can belong to one project. Your Machine picks it when its thread or the people it's about are on a single open project, and the to-do shows its name under the title. **Project** under the to-do's **Details**, or **Put on a project** in the command menu (Ctrl K), changes it or takes it off. Your choice stays.

A project's page lists its to-dos under **To-dos**, open ones first, and the board says how many are open. Linking a to-do to a shared project shares nothing more: your team still sees only what came from shared messages.

## A project's organization

Each project can be linked to one organization. Its page, the list and the board show it, with a link.

When nobody picked one, your Machine sets it from the people on the project and its messages, leaving out your colleagues. It only does that when one organization is clearly the other side: personal email like Gmail never counts, and a project with several companies stays without one. The project form then says "From its people".

Your pick in **Edit** always stays, also when you take it off. **Same organization as…** moves an organization's projects along, and **Split off** moves them back.

## Boards

Projects of one kind in stages, like an evaluation pipeline. **⋯** on a board: **Edit board** (name, stages, which projects belong), **Open on this board** and **New board**.

Checks keep adding projects that fit and place them in a stage. When they aren't sure, the board shows "2 projects could belong here": **Add** or **Leave out**. Tick a few, or **Select all**, to do them at once, and **Undo** right after if you change your mind. A project that bundles several counterparts becomes one project per counterpart when you add it.

A new board, or a suggested board you save, looks through your whole history once. It makes one project per organization that fits and puts each in a stage. The header says "Looking through your history…" while it works, then how many it added and how many wait for you. An Only-you board keeps what it finds to you.

**Follow-ups…** in a board's **⋯** follows up by itself when someone doesn't reply. See [Follow-ups](follow-ups.md).

## Done when

**Done when**, under **Edit** on a project, says what finishes it in plain words, with its proof: **A live page** (The Machine opens the link from their message and checks it links to you) or **Their message**. Checks suggest one for partner listings.

A check only moves a project to the last stage with that proof, linked on its page; **Won** by hand still works. The page also shows what holds it up (no reply in 10+ days, an overdue promise) and adds one to-do for it when it has none. Nothing is sent without you.

## Pipeline

**Pipeline**, at the top of a board, adds up the value of its open projects per stage, and what's expected to close per month from their close dates. Won, lost and stalled projects aren't open; what you won this month shows apart.

Values are in euros unless your Machine is set to another currency. With **Owner** in Filter, it counts only yours.

## A teammate's board

In **Team**, boards your teammates share show next to yours, the ones with most of your projects first. Each project says what happened last, with its date: the newest line shared with the team, else its last move. Its owner's name shows when a stage has projects of more than one teammate; when one person owns the whole board, the header says so.

**Mine**, **Needs me** and **All** pick what a shared board shows, and the Team list of projects with it. Mine is what you're on: a thread of it, a project you own, or a to-do of yours about it. Needs me is what waits on you: an open to-do about it, or a thread where the newest message was written to you.

The rest of a stage folds into **Show N more**. Projects that look alike, like a run of "… CRM evaluation" trials, fold into one row with a count; tap it to open them. None of this changes what's shared.
