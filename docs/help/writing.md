## Signatures

In **Settings**, **Writing**, each mail account has a signature for the emails you send. **Import from Gmail** takes the one Gmail has; for Outlook, **Find in sent mail** finds it in your sent mail. Change it as you like, then press **Save**, which shows as soon as you change something.

## How you write

A few lines per channel, like Email or Slack, plus one for everywhere. Drafts follow them. They update by themselves from your messages, notes and edits. Change one and **Save**, or **Remove** it.

The quickest way to teach it: edit a draft before sending. It learns from what you changed.

It learns only from what you wrote yourself. A draft sent as is never counts as your writing.

### It keeps learning

About twice a day, when there's enough new, it looks at drafts you changed, to-dos you dismissed or snoozed, and corrections in a to-do's chat. Then it makes a few small updates to how you write, your rules and facts. These checks wait when your plan's limits run low.

**Learning**, **Changes** shows each update and why. **Undo** reverses it. Lines you wrote yourself stay as you wrote them.

### When a line is wrong

Fix it where you see it:

- In **Settings**, **Writing**, change the words in that channel's box and **Save**. Your wording then stays.
- Or say it in any to-do's chat, like "I only lowercase the start of sentences, not names". The chat shows **Saved to How you write**, with **Edit**.
- **Learning**, **What I've learned** lists every line too, each with **Edit**.

Be exact: drafts follow the lines word for word. "Lowercase" alone also lowercases names; "sentences start lowercase, names keep their capitals" doesn't. Habits stay on their channel, so a chat habit never reaches your emails.
