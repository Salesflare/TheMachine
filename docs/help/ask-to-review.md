## Ask someone to review a to-do

1. On the to-do, open **More** and pick **Ask to review**. It works at any step: before Do it, on a ready draft, or just before you send.
2. Pick who. A Work to-do goes only to someone in your Work Fleet, a Personal one only to someone in your Family.
3. Check what goes: the draft (or the to-do's text) and its sources. Leave a source out with ×. Add a note if you like, then pick **Ask**.

Only that to-do goes, nothing else from your Machine. The to-do now says "Waiting for Pat's review", with **Withdraw** to take the request back.

## Reviewing for someone else

Their request shows up as a to-do of your own: "Review: Reply to Sam about pricing". Edit the draft right there, add a comment, then pick **Approve**, **Send changes**, **Send comment** or **Decline**.

Your Machine can help in the to-do's chat, but a review to-do never sends anything. Your review goes back to its owner, and they decide what goes out.

## Their changes come back

The to-do says what happened: "Pat approved", "Pat commented" or "Pat suggested 3 changes", with their comment. Pick **See changes** to see each change, marked in the text, with who made it and when.

- **Accept all** puts every change in your draft.
- **Accept selected** takes only the ticked ones.
- **Ignore** leaves the draft as it was.

If you edited the draft in the meantime, changes go in where they still fit. Accepting only changes the draft: nothing is sent until you press Send yourself. The to-do's chat keeps each step ("Asked Pat to review", "Pat suggested 3 changes", "Accepted 2 of Pat's changes").

One review per to-do at a time. Once it's back, declined or withdrawn, you can ask again, the same person or someone else. A request nobody answers expires after 3 days.
