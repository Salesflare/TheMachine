## Your Machine

- On your own computer: free. Everything The Machine does, with your own model.
- Hosted: {price.standard} a month for a Standard Machine, or {price.big} for a Bigger one, excl. VAT. The first {trial_days} days are free, once per person, and nothing is charged before they end. If yours started without a card, add one on your account page before then.

Your AI plan is billed by your provider, like OpenAI. We add nothing to it.

## On the Network

- Friends and Family: free, with any Machine. In a Family Fleet, hosted Machines cost {price.family_pair} for the first two together, then {price.family_seat} each, excl. VAT.
- Work Fleet: {price.seat} a seat a month, or {price.seat_big} for a Bigger one, excl. VAT. A teammate who runs their own Machine is {price.network} a month, and one who's already hosted is free. Each Fleet has its own card and invoices, and the owner confirms every amount before it's charged.

All prices are per month, excl. VAT. See [Pricing](/pricing).

## Card and invoices

In **Settings** › **Account**, press **Billing**, then **Manage subscription**. It opens Stripe, where you change your card and download invoices. Your card details never reach us.

Change your Machine's size any time: in **Settings** › **Account**, press **Change plan**.
