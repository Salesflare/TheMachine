## Turn it on

GBrain is an open-source agent memory some people already run. It's off unless you turn it on, and only on a Machine on your own computer. Install GBrain first, then open **Connections**, **+ Add**, **GBrain**.

Your Machine says what goes in: people and projects from your Graph with a link back, and what it learned about how you work. Message text never goes in. GBrain may send this to its own AI providers. Check **I agree**, then **Connect GBrain**.

## What it does

Reviews can read your brain (search and read pages, never write) to write better to-dos. Your Machine writes its part of each page between clear markers. Edit or remove that part and it leaves it as you made it. Notes you write about a person in GBrain show up next to them in your Graph.

Work people and companies go in only when you check **Include Work people and companies**. Teammates' Team Graph data never goes in.

## Turn it off

Open **GBrain** again and press **Turn off**. Your brain and its pages stay. Your Machine removes only its own link to GBrain and the notes it brought back.
