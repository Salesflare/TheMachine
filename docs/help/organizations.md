Companies, from the addresses and names in your messages. Their industry, size, place and logo are looked up: see [Organization details](organization-data.md).

## An organization's page

It's built like a person's: its logo, name and what it does, one line on how you're in touch, then **Contact** (its website and LinkedIn), **With you** (what it is to you), the people there and its **Timeline**.

Its **Projects** list open ones first, each with its stage. Its timeline has its people's messages, the Intercom conversations from its domain and its projects' moves. See [Timeline](people.md#timeline).

**Ask the Fleet who knows** it finds a way in through your Fleet.

## Add an organization

**New organization** at the top of Organizations, or **+** in the top bar on a phone. Give a name and its website. When one like it is already in your Graph, the form shows it first, so you can open that one instead. **Add** opens the new page.

An organization you added can be deleted from its **⋯** menu while nobody is in it.

## Edit an organization

**Edit** at the top changes its details and what it is to you. To change its logo, click it: **Upload a picture**, pick one found for it, or **Remove picture**.

## Combine two organizations

Organizations that look like doubles show under **Same organization?**: **Merge** or **Not the same**.

To do it yourself, **⋯**, **Same organization as…** on an organization's page makes it part of another one, with its people, projects and notes.

**Add a website…** adds one more of its websites, like its .io next to its .com. People with an address there join it, and its details are looked up for every website.

They're listed under **Websites**, each with **Split off** to undo it. Later edits to the combined organization are kept.

## Warm-up mail

Mail from email warm-up tools is kept out, under "Hidden as warm-up", with **Undo** per domain.
