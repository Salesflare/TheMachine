# Product vision

AI should work for you: proactive, on your side and yours alone. It takes work off your hands, so you have time for your best work and your best life. The Machine is that AI:

- **Yours, and only yours.** Your AI and your data belong to you. No nation, corporation or anyone else
  controls them. Only you do.
- **Proactive.** You shouldn't have to figure out how AI can help; it figures that out and takes work off your hands
  every day.
- **With you everywhere.** It follows you seamlessly across your computer, your phone and every app you use.
- **Work and life, as one.** You're one person, not two. It sees your whole day, and keeps private what's private.
- **Always current.** It keeps pace with AI: new models and abilities reach your Machine as soon as they're good.
- **Secure, wherever it runs.** On your own computer for complete ownership, or on a private cloud computer we run
  for you when you'd rather not think about it.
- **For everyone.** AI shouldn't only lift up technical people. It should lift up everyone, equally: easy to start,
  affordable, in your own language.

This page says where we're going and what "best" means. Every change, agent and test is judged against it. It is
reviewed every few days and changes only on the owner's word.

## The idea

Your day shouldn't be spent sorting mail, chasing replies and remembering what you promised. Without being asked,
The Machine reads everything that comes in (mail, Slack, WhatsApp, Telegram, calendar) and hands you a short list of
to-dos with the work already done: the draft written, the meeting prepped, the promise kept track of. You decide, it
does. Then it gets out of the way. Success is less time in the app and more time for the people and the work that
matter.

## What we believe, in order

1. **It's yours, and only yours.** Only you control your AI and your data, not a nation, a company or anyone
   else. It runs on your own
   computer, or on a private cloud computer of your own that we host, in open source code you can read. You pick the
   model (OpenAI, Claude, a local one) and use your own plan. It outlives any provider: your Machine and everything it
   learned stay yours, in open formats you can take with you, even if a model or a company disappears. Nothing goes
   out without you seeing it, and security is never relaxed.
2. **It's right, and honest about it.** No to-do is missed: it catches every to-do, promise and follow-up that
   matters, and lets the noise go. It shows where things come from, says when it's unsure, and never makes things
   up.
3. **It does the work, you keep control.** Its agents do as much as they can on their own, without you ever losing
   control. You never have to work out what to ask; it spots what it can help with and offers it. Every to-do arrives
   as close to done as it can be: one tap to approve, not homework. It starts by asking and earns more freedom as it
   proves itself. You can always see what it did, and undo it.
4. **It's for everyone.** Easy: set up in minutes, no technical skills needed, plain words in your own language.
   Affordable: a fair price for a hosted Machine, and free to run yourself.
5. **It brings you closer to people.** It helps you show up for the people in your life: remember, follow up, keep
   your word. It writes in your voice, but never pretends to be you behind your back.
6. **It's with you everywhere.** Desktop, phone, chat apps, browser: one Machine that carries the context from one to
   the next and works with the tools you already use. Work and personal stay apart where they should (your team
   never sees your personal life) and come together where it helps.
7. **It feels instant and calm.** Every click answers in about 100 ms. It shows rather than tells: chat and plain
   text are flexible, but rarely the quickest way to take something in. Few words, few notifications, only ones
   that matter. It asks for less of your attention, not more.
8. **It's smart, not wasteful.** As much intelligence as possible per token, so it runs well on a $20 OpenAI or Claude
   plan. Model calls go where they change the outcome; plain code, caching and cheap checks do the rest.
9. **It gets better by itself, for you and for everyone.** It learns you fast, without being taught. The whole
   system improves too: each Machine judges changes for its own person, and only what helps spreads.
10. **It works for teams and families.** A Fleet shares context, and the Graph and Boards become the CRM that
    replaces Salesflare.

## What it isn't

Not a chatbot that waits for you to prompt it. Not a dashboard of everything. Not an app that competes for your
attention. Not something that acts behind your back or speaks for you unseen. Not a walled garden that holds your
data or your choice of model hostage.

## How we get there: fast learning, no churn

- Bugs and misses are fixed the same day. People feel it getting better, not moving around.
- Names, layout and flows stay stable. Changes are small and one at a time; anything big goes to the owner first.
- Progress is measured: fewer missed and wrong to-dos, a faster first to-do, less friction, faster clicks and fewer tokens, week over week.
